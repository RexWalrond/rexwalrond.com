About Rex Walrond

Theodore Rex Walrond, who goes by Rex, is a University of Florida neuroscience graduate whose work has moved from the research lab toward healthcare and finance. He is currently building an early-stage venture in consumer health.

Education

University of Florida: B.S. in Psychology, Behavioral and Cognitive Neuroscience, cum laude (2025)
University of Notre Dame: completed graduate coursework in finance

Research background

McKnight Brain Institute, University of Florida

Neuroscience Researcher, 2022–2023

Rex conducted literature reviews and statistical analyses across more than 300 studies on subarachnoid hemorrhage and Alzheimer's disease. He developed an analytical framework identifying variables that link vascular trauma to neurodegeneration, helping the team prioritize future experiments, and presented findings to faculty and researchers across three departments.

Romark Laboratories

Research Assistant, Summer 2021

Rex built Excel dashboards tracking more than 25 COVID-19 drug candidates by efficacy, trial phase and mechanism, and synthesized over 400 articles and multi-drug datasets to evaluate the antiviral potential of niclosamide.

Service

American Parkinson Disease Association

Impact Partner, 2025–2026

Rex led a monthly Parkinson's disease support group of 15 to 20 participants, planning and running each session, coordinating guest speakers, facilitating caregiver-focused conversations and reporting on APDA outreach.

University of Florida Scientific Diving Program

Divemaster, 2020–2023

Rex helped teach scuba courses from Open Water through Master Diver, covering in-water and out-of-water skills and classroom lectures on dive planning, dive physics and physiology, for more than 30 students across the program's certification courses. He holds SDI certifications through Divemaster.

Outside of work

Rex spends as much time as he can at higher elevations or below the surface: mountaineering, backpacking, skiing and diving. He has summited Mount Rainier and covered long stretches of the John Muir Trail, the Teton Crest Trail and Glacier National Park's backcountry. He also plays golf and acoustic guitar, and tracks his training, nutrition and sleep data more closely than strictly necessary. Trip reports, gear notes and an ever-changing ranking of Zach Bryan's discography live on his personal site.
